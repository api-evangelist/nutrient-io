Nutrient announces Documents for Salesforce, a natively built solution that consolidates document generation, editing, redaction, and signing inside Salesforce. The platform eliminates fragmented document workflows by keeping templates, editing, and signing on the Salesforce record — governed by existing CRM permissions and controls.
